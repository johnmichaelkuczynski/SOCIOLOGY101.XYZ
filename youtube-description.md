## 📝 Description (paste into YouTube)

Sociology 101 is a complete, four-week introduction to sociology that teaches, tutors, drills, and grades itself, entirely with AI. It builds the discipline from the ground up: the sociological perspective and imagination, the founding thinkers and major theoretical frameworks, how sociologists actually do research, culture and the social construction of the self, socialization and everyday interaction, inequality across class, race, and gender, deviance and social control, and the institutions and forces that drive social change.

In this video you'll see the whole course in action:

🔎 Twenty-eight topics across four weeks — from the sociological imagination all the way to social change
📖 Three-depth lessons — read any topic Short, Medium, or Long, the same material at the depth you want
💬 A section-scoped AI tutor that answers questions about the exact paragraph you're reading
🎯 Adaptive practice that gets harder when you're on a streak and eases off when you're stuck
📝 AI-graded homework, tests, and a final — each with a written rationale and a percent score
🛡️ Two-layer AI-authorship detection on every submission (a text classifier AND a keystroke-pattern detector)
📊 Live analytics and one-click operator diagnostics that verify the whole stack end-to-end

Built for adults taking up the subject — undergraduates, graduate students, researchers, and faculty — as well as instructors evaluating AI-taught coursework and academic-integrity researchers.
